Head, Strategy & Growth

Dr. Himanshu Kundra

Dr. Himanshu Kundra is an education leader with over 15 years of experience across the education sector, spanning business strategy, institutional growth, marketing, admissions, and stakeholder engagement. His experience across schools, colleges, universities, and education businesses gives him a deep understanding of the challenges facing educational institutions and the opportunities to create meaningful, sustainable impact.

At ThinkHumanly, Himanshu leads growth initiatives and strategic partnerships, focusing on expanding the organisation's presence among schools and educational institutions. He works towards building strategic relationships and creating opportunities to bring human-centric AI literacy, critical thinking, and essential human skills to a wider audience.

Passionate about the future of education, Himanshu believes that preparing students for an AI-driven world requires more than technological proficiency. It demands the ability to think critically, question intelligently, make informed decisions, and use technology responsibly.

Through his work at ThinkHumanly, he aims to help educational institutions embrace this vision and empower the next generation with the skills and judgment they need to thrive in an increasingly technology-driven world.
